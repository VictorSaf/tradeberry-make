Intrinsic time

It works on any FX pair; on a CFD it shows a message and an Open EUR/USD button. A busy hour holds many steps of intrinsic time, a quiet one few.

Controls

ControlOptions · defaultEffect
Threshold δ0.05% · 0.10% · 0.20% · 0.50%The reversal that counts as an event. Small δ = many small events (a busy clock); large δ = only the big swings. Every number on the page is measured with this δ.
AutoAuto x.xxx%δ chosen for this instrument so that the history gives about 1.5 events per hour of trading (between 1 and 2); its value is shown on the button. Not kept after a reload; picking a fixed δ clears it.
05

How it is computed

event price ≤ last high × (1 − δ) → down · price ≥ last low × (1 + δ) → up on all 1-minute closes
the first event is discarded (its extreme depends on the arbitrary start)
rate events ÷ hours of trading closed hours count zero
overshoot |ln(next extreme ÷ event price)| ÷ δ · mean ± 1.96 SE
heartbeat events in the last 6 hours ÷ λ, λ = rate × 6 · band = central 95% of Poisson(λ)
coastline Σ |ln(extreme ÷ previous extreme)| in % of price, against the net move
reversal current extreme × (1 ∓ δ) · avg leg end = event price × (1 ± δ × avg overshoot)
Fewer than 4 events: “Only N directional changes at δ — pick a smaller threshold.”
06

The eight figures

FigureRead it as
Reversal levelA δ move back from the current extreme would confirm the opposite leg at this price. A natural place for an alert or a stop.
EventsReversals of δ per hour of open market. More per hour = busier, choppier at this scale.
HeartbeatThe last 6 hours against the average pace. Highlighted only outside the Poisson band; inside it is normal variation. London and New York hours bring more events than Asia.
Avg overshootHow much further price went after a reversal was confirmed, in δ, with its 95% interval. FX research finds about 1 × δ.
CoastlineHow far price travelled to get where it is. A long coastline with a small net move = a lot of travel for little progress: a range.
Last legWhich way the last reversal went, nothing more.
Overshoot so farHow far the current leg has run past its confirmation, ranked against past overshoots. A description, not a turning signal.
Average leg ends atA price, not a forecast: where the current leg would end if it were exactly average. Legs end early or run long at random.

Charts and the action row

  • Intrinsic-time chart: the last 80 events (or all). Each dot is a change point, each thick segment its overshoot (green up, red down). On the right: now, reversal (dashed) and avg leg (dotted), scale references, not targets. The time axis shows the UTC time of the extremes at even steps, so the uneven gaps are intentional. Hover an event for its extreme, confirmation and overshoot.
  • Alert at reversal: adds a price alert at the reversal level, below the price after an up leg, above it after a down leg, in the Alerts list like one set from the chart. The level is recomputed at the moment you click. It fires if price crosses it; it is not an order.
08

Scaling law

Events are also counted at 2δ and 4δ. For a random walk the count falls about 4× each time δ doubles. A ratio is read against the rough noise of the two counts.

δEventsRatioReading (illustration)
0.10%384—the base
0.20%1013.80near 4: as a random walk scales
0.40%244.21near 4: as a random walk scales

Clearly above 4: fewer reversals at the larger δ than a random walk gives (moves stall before getting large). Clearly below 4: more big swings than a random walk (trending at larger scales). Under 10 events at the larger δ: too few to read.

Pick δ for your trades

0.05–0.10% for intraday, 0.20–0.50% for swings. A δ close to the moves you trade makes every number speak your scale.

Alert, don’t stare

Set Alert at reversal and decide when it fires: at that moment the current leg is over by this definition.

Stops and the heartbeat

Avoid tight stops when the heartbeat is high: small reversals are frequent.

Limits

The method describes this week’s history. A long leg by this history is a description, not a reason to fade it; how long a leg lasts or how far it runs is not predicted.
