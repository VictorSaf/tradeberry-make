The question

The question

“Does the market revisit the same states? Each pixel compares two moments; lines and blocks show repeating sequences and stuck phases.”

02

What you see

03

Controls

ControlOptions · defaultEffect
State size5m · 15m · 1HOne state per 5-minute, 15-minute or 1-hour bar. Larger bars cover more days with the same picture size.
ModeDistance · RecurrencesEvery pixel shaded by how similar the two moments are (only the closest half of all pairs is shaded) · only the 10% most similar pairs of moments, the ones the statistics count.
04

How it is computed

bars the last 260 bars of the chosen size
returns ÷ the usual size of a move at its UTC hour, then ÷ their SD hour-of-day rhythm divided out
state 3 consecutive normalised returns (embedding dimension 3)
ε the distance that makes the closest 10% of pairs recurrences recurrence rate 10%
DET share of recurrences on diagonal lines of length ≥ 2
LAM share of recurrences in vertical lines of length ≥ 2
band 120 shuffles of the same returns at the same ε · 90% band, and a corrected band of 5% ÷ 3 per side
The corrected band (1.67%–98.33%) accounts for the three checks read: determinism high, determinism low, laminarity high. Under 200 states the tool warns that the measures move a lot from one window to the next.
05

How to read it: the verdict

VerdictMeaning, in the tool’s words
Repeating sequencesSequences of states repeat more than in the shuffles: most likely calm and busy stretches coming back, not a direction.
Stuck phasesThe market stays in one state more than in the shuffles: stretches of similar, usually calm, moves.
Unusually novel movesFewer repeating sequences than in the shuffles: recent moves did not look like each other.
BorderlineOutside the 90% band of the shuffles but inside the corrected one: too close to call structure.
No structure beyond chanceBoth measures inside the band: the order of the moves looks random.
06

The other cards

CardRule
Regime nowThe last 24 states (6 hours at 15m, 2 hours at 5m, 24 hours at 1H) against every 24-state stretch of the window. Recurring least (bottom 10%): Busier than usual if their moves run above 1.1× the window’s average size, else Unusual states. Recurring most (top 10%): Calmer than usual if below 0.9×, else Unusual states. Otherwise Usual.
Determinism · LaminarityEach value with the shuffled band and the corrected band, and one line: above, below, borderline or inside. Highlighted when above.
Now resemblesUp to 3 earlier states closest to the current one (at least 4 states apart), their distance in thresholds (under 1× is a recurrence) and the range of the next 8 bars: sizes, not directions. Click a row to mark it on the plot.
Pace, not direction

Use the plot for pace and phases. Stuck phases above the band: quiet or busy phases persist, so expect the current pace to continue a while.

Size stops from analogues

The “Now resembles” ranges are how far price swung after similar states: a reference for stop distance over the next hours, never for which way.

Limits

Bright streaks are most likely similar volatility, not a repeated direction: the states hold the shape and relative size of three bars, not the price. Use it for pace and phases, never as a direction signal.
