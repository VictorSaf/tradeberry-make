Tapestry

Each of the 16 pairs is a horizon chart: its line, folded into coloured bands so that 16 rows fit in the space of one chart. Under the rows, the calendar’s releases and the four FX sessions share the same time axis.

Controls

ControlOptions · defaultEffect

04

How it is computed

move v = ln(close ÷ close at the window start) per row; also shown in pips or points
band, common = max |v| over all rows ÷ 3
band, per pair = σ(1-min returns) × √bars ÷ 3
3 shades each side · green above the start, red below
Cluster order: a walk through the Constellation tree of the same window (1-minute returns), starting at the open pair, nearest neighbours first. Gaps over 3 hours are a closed market, drawn as a dashed line labelled “weekend”.

The theme (All): the strongest and the weakest currency of the window against the average of the eight (the same strength as the rest of the terminal), with how many of its pairs on the rows moved that way. Fewer than three in four agree: mixed, shown muted, “a mixed picture, not a theme across its pairs.”

News lane: the releases of the terminal’s calendar inside the window, for currencies on the rows. It keeps its last 8 releases. Diamonds are sized by impact; high and medium ones also draw a dotted line through the rows.

05

How to read it

You seeIt means
Several rows change colour at the same momentThey moved together: that is when the theme started.
A theme marked mixedA single-pair story, not a currency theme.

Hover a row for that moment: the pair’s move there and now, its high and low with times, and the top and bottom three pairs at that moment. Click a row to open the pair.

The day’s story

Use 1D and sort by Return: winners on top, losers at the bottom, and the time each move began.

Themes as blocks

Sort by Cluster: pairs that move together sit together, so a currency theme shows as one block of colour.

Test your view

Pick the currency you have a view on: if all its pairs agree, the view is broad; if one pair carries it, it is a single-pair story.

Limits

Rows show where price stands against the window’s start, so the picture depends on that start: change the window and a “winner” can become flat. The theme describes the window; it does not forecast. The news lane shows only the calendar’s last 8 releases.
