01

The question

The question

“Has each pair's recent path been persistent, anti-persistent or like noise over 1–32 minutes? Every pair is placed by its memory (Hurst) and its randomness (entropy), against the band where the same returns with random signs land. It describes the window; it does not forecast.”

02

What you see

Across: the Hurst exponent, the memory of the path. Up and down: permutation entropy, how random the order of the moves was. Dashed lines mark the band edges and Hurst 0.5. Dot colour is the move over the whole window (deeper up to 30 bp), dot size the volatility. Each dot is a target: hover it for its numbers, click it (or press Enter) to open the pair.

03

Controls

ControlOptions · defaultEffect
Window4H · 1D · 3DThe last 240, 1,440 or 4,320 one-minute bars. Hurst on their 1-minute returns; entropy on their 5-minute returns, averaged over the five ways to cut the 5-minute grid. Shared with the Constellation.
04

How it is computed

Hurst Var(sum of k returns) for k = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 (at least 12 sums per level)
H = slope of ln Var against ln k ÷ 2, kept in 0…1 needs ≥ 48 returns
Entropy 4-point up/down patterns of 5-minute returns, ties skipped
PE = Shannon entropy ÷ ln 24, mean of the 5 grid offsets ≥ 24 patterns each; 145 bars
Efficiency ER = |Σ r| ÷ Σ |r| 100% = a straight line
Band 160 copies with each return’s sign flipped at random → 5th–95th percentile of H, PE, ER
A pair with fewer than half its 1-minute returns non-zero is not measured (“its quotes barely moved in the window”); so is one without enough bars free of a closed market.
05

Zones and labels

ConditionThe tool writes
H above the bandpersistent over 1–32 minutes (above the noise band: its moves ran on more than chance)
H inside the bandwithin the noise band — random-walk-like over 1–32 minutes
H below the bandanti-persistent over 1–32 minutes (below the noise band: its moves were undone more than chance)
PE below its bandbelow the noise band — some repeating micro-patterns
PE otherwisewithin the noise band — indistinguishable from noise

Entropy only deviates downwards: noise is already as random as it gets. The zone (and its tint) is set by Hurst alone.

Match the style

Persistent + low entropy + higher efficiency = a cleaner trend in the window; left of the band = chopping. Most pairs inside: the market is mostly noise right now.

Check the change

Look at 1D first; switch to 4H to see whether a pair has just changed character. A reading that holds on all three windows is structure.

Describe, then plan

Use the map to describe character, then let your plan decide entries and exits.

Limits

A point outside the band describes the recent path; it does not predict the next move. Measured noise sits at an entropy a little below 1 and around, not exactly at, a Hurst of 0.5: that is why the band, not the textbook value, is the reference.
