04

How it is computed

activity |1-minute log return| in bp, returns across a gap left out
petal median, over the previous UTC days, of each day’s average activity in that 15-minute slot one agitated day barely moves it
colour petal ÷ day average > 1.3 orange · < 0.7 blue · else grey
today from the FX rollover at 17:00 New York (21:00 UTC summer, 22:00 winter); a CFD’s current session
now last 60 minutes ÷ the usual of the same slots needs ≥ 15 one-minute bars
next hour σ per minute = petal × √(π/2) · 1σ = √(Σ σ²) over the open minutes · 2σ = 2 × 1σ
Worked example

The next hour’s petals average 0.40 pips per minute. σ per minute = 0.40 × 1.2533 = 0.501; over 60 open minutes, 1σ = 0.501 × √60 = ±3.9 pips, about two hours in three; 2σ = ±7.8 pips, 95% on a normal curve. A 20-pip ticket stop is 5.2× the next hour’s 1σ.

Before placing a stop

Know whether activity is about to rise or fade. A stop of only 1–2× the next hour’s 1σ is within normal noise.

Entries and activity

Place entries before activity rises if you need movement; avoid tight stops just before it.

Today against usual

Much busier than usual: something is moving this market. Much quieter: expect small ranges.

Limits

The profile comes from the week of 1-minute bars kept; with fewer than 3 days in a slot the median is unsteady and the tool says so. The clock measures size, never direction. Release days can make any slot behave unlike its petal.
