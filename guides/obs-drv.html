Drivers

If the quote currency did the moving, a view on the base currency is the wrong reason for this trade. Each currency is measured against an equal-weighted basket of all eight, so a pair’s move splits exactly into a base leg and a quote leg.

Observatory guide
01

The question

The question

“Which currency moves the pair. The pair split into its two currencies, each measured against all eight.”

02

What you see

Under the verdict: a table of Legs by window (1H, 4H, 1D, 3D, as far as the history reaches: base leg, quote leg, pair in bp and pips, led by), a chart of the two legs and the pair cumulative over the last 24 trading hours (hover for the values at any moment), and on the right the Currency board and the Widest strength gap.

03

Controls

ControlOptions · defaultEffect
window1H · 4H · 1DThe span the board, the verdict and the earlier windows it is compared with are measured over (a weekend is skipped). 1H is the noisiest; the history holds only a few earlier 1-day windows. Not kept after a reload.
04

How it is computed

value of C ln(C in USD) from each USD pair’s 1-minute closes USD = 0
basket mean of the 8 log-values
leg of C Δ ln C − Δ basket over the window
base leg leg(base) · quote leg = −leg(quote) · base leg + quote leg = pair move
leader share = |base| ÷ (|base| + |quote|) · a leader when one side > 60% and its leg > 1 bp
A currency outside the eight (an exotic with one G8 leg) is measured against USD through its USD pair, shown under “Outside the eight · not in the basket”, and left out of the basket, the strongest, the weakest and the gap.
05

How to read it: the verdict

StoryWhenThe sentence
<C>-ledOne side > 60% and > 1 bp“Over the last 4 hours of trading, EUR/USD is mostly a EUR story: 81% of the move comes from EUR strength against the basket.”
SharedNo leaderUp to 55%: “both legs … contribute about equally … a shared move.” Above: “the move is shared: X brings s% of it, under the 60% that names a leader.”
OffsettingLegs of opposite signs“… the two legs pulled in opposite directions … They [partly] offset, leaving the pair z.”
SmallBoth legs within 1 bp“… too small to say which currency moved it.”

Two more lines can follow. A turn: on the 4H and 1D windows, if a different currency led the last hour and the pair moved more than 3 bp in it: “In the last hour of trading JPY took over …”. The usual leader: earlier windows of the same length (up to 30, at least 4 needed), e.g. “Before this window: usually EUR, which led 9 of the 14 earlier 4-hour windows of trading; USD led 3, neither 2.”

06

Cleaner expression

When one currency led, the tool names the pair that isolates it best now: the leader against the currency that moved least against the basket.

From the board on the right

“EUR led: EUR/CHF isolates it best now — its other currency, CHF, moved least against the basket over the last 4 hours of trading (−1.0 bp), so that pair moves mostly with EUR. Describes the window, not a trade idea.”

The widest strength gap on the same board is EUR against JPY: EUR/JPY, EUR stronger than JPY by 47.0 bp. “A past gap is not a forecast of the next move.”

Check your reason

You buy EUR/USD because you like the euro. If the verdict says USD-led, the trade is really a dollar trade.

Look for one dominant leg

In Legs by window, a window where one leg dominates names the story. Where the cumulative lines separate is when one currency took over.

Express it cleanly

Click the cleaner-expression pill to open that pair: it carries the driver with less noise from the second currency.

Limits

Drivers splits a currency pair; a CFD, or a pair with neither currency among the eight, shows a message and buttons for EUR/USD, USD/JPY, GBP/USD and AUD/USD instead. Everything describes the window; a strong currency in the past hours is not a forecast.
