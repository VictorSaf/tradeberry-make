Operational rules

A compact reference for implementation and review.

Solid yellowOne primary neutral action per region; small ON controls.
Soft tintSelected option, row or segmented choice.
Yellow lineCurrent tab, destination or timeframe.
Yellow textLink, disclosure or secondary action on hover.
Yellow ringKeyboard focus and direct manipulation target.
Never yellowBuy, Sell, destructive actions, profit, loss and warning.
