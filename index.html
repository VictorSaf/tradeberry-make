What makes it different

  • Risk-first ticket: size by % of equity, see $ risk, R:R and margin before you click.
  • Drag SL/TP directly on the chart — for the ticket and for open positions.
  • Correlation guard warns when a new trade stacks exposure you already hold.
  • Net currency exposure decomposes every position into its underlying currencies.
  • Natural-language command bar for execution, alerts and navigation.
  • Risk Lab stress-tests your journal stats with 1,000 Monte Carlo paths.
  • Time compression up to 300× to rehearse a full session in minutes.
  • Multi-chart layouts (up to 2 × 2) with synced crosshair, timeframe link, one-click trading in every pane and auto-fill with correlated pairs.
  • Drawing tools — trendlines, rays, channels, Fibonacci retracements and extensions — anchored in time so they survive timeframe changes; trendlines can fire alerts.
  • Strategy backtester with minute-level fills, out-of-sample check, parameter heatmap and all-pairs robustness test.
  • Market Brief: an analyst-style write-up for every pair, with a bias scanner, key levels and a one-click trade plan.
  • Observatory: correlation constellation, regime map (Hurst × entropy), intrinsic-time event chart, historical analogues with an honest hit-rate check, recurrence plot, currency drivers, volatility clock and a horizon-chart tapestry of all pairs.
  • Behaviour: a pre-mortem before risky trades (with red flags found automatically and tracking of trades you skipped) and a disposition-effect meter (Odean's PGR vs PLR).
  • Baskets: build any weighted currency index, chart it, find its closest pair and trade it as one set of legs. Intrinsic-time candles: one candle per δ move.
  • Listen to the market: sonified ticks and a portfolio heartbeat; the ticket shows random-walk odds of hitting TP before SL and the typical time to resolve.
  • Depth-of-market ladder: one click places a limit or stop order at that exact price.
  • Trade coach reads your history and flags revenge trades, loss aversion and your best pairs and sessions.

SIM Prices are simulated (a zero-drift random walk starting from a broker platform snapshot of 2026-10-20). Directional edge is zero by construction; analytics here measure risk, behaviour and luck.

Volatility follows the trading sessions and currencies move together through shared factors. This is a demo environment — no real money is at risk.
